Air conditioning

Signs your AC needs a check

Any of these can point to a car AC problem worth diagnosing before it gets worse.

Weak or no cooling

The AC runs but the cabin doesn't get properly cold, even at full setting.

Low airflow

Air barely comes out of the vents, or airflow feels much weaker than before.

Unusual noises

Rattling, squealing or clicking sounds when the AC or compressor is running.

Odd smells

Musty, sour or burning smells coming from the vents when the AC is on.

These symptoms can have several different causes — bring the car in and we'll diagnose it properly before any repair.
